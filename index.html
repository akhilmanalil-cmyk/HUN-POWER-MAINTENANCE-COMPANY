About HPMC Group

Three companies. One engineering standard.

HPMC Group is an industrial holding structure bringing together three independently operating companies — each rooted in its own market, each held to the same engineering discipline. Hun Power Maintenance Company anchors the Group's presence in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Avram Apex Gulf Industrial & Contracting serves the Kingdom of Bahrain, and ABK Industrial & Contracting operates from India across manufacturing, EPC and procurement.

Together, the Group covers the full lifecycle of industrial assets — from oil filtration and mechanical maintenance through fabrication, commissioning and full EPC delivery — for clients in oil & gas, petrochemical, power generation, marine, steel and manufacturing.
